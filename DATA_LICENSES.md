# Data provenance

## Motor thrust curves

The `.eng` files under `l2_engine/motors/` were extracted from the OpenRocket
24.12 bundled motor database at upstream commit
`6e18add7055a66dcae580f9d1e0dc1538c0454c7`.

They are distributed as non-compilable data alongside this GPL-covered work
under OpenRocket's additional permission under GPL version 3 section 7. The
relevant permission and full license text are included in
`licenses/OpenRocket-LICENSE.txt`.

The files may include factual manufacturer and motor identifiers. No trademark
ownership or manufacturer endorsement is claimed.

## Competition and example artifacts

OSIFOG examples are retained as project-authored validation evidence. Regulation
PDFs, third-party rocket designs, game assets, mod assets, downloaded catalogs,
and simulator installations are not covered by the project license merely
because they have existed in a development workspace. They must not be included
in a public release without a separate provenance and redistribution review.
