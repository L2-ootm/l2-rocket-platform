# OSIFOG 2026 reference

OSIFOG 2026 is the completed competition case that proved the platform under a
real scoring and legality constraint.

The preserved artifacts currently remain at:

- `designs/osifog_finalization/` — final submitted package and verification;
- `designs/osifog_submission/` — candidate comparison audit trail;
- `designs/osifog_level3/` — selected design references and reports;
- `missions/osifog_l3_precision.json` — mission constraints and scoring.

These artifacts are examples and regression evidence. They are not the platform
architecture, and new engine capabilities must not be hard-coded to OSIFOG
rules.
